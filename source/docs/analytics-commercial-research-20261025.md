# Initial commercial offer and market dependencies

Research date: 2026-10-25 America/New_York. Primary public sources only. No vendor
was contacted, application submitted, agreement signed, buyer represented as
committed, account opened or expenditure authorized by this note.

Decision: start with one contextual sponsorship slot and a visitor-requested
introduction on AEKR's public integration/content surface. The private marKER
owner console is a campaign consumer, not an invented mass audience. Actual
visitor volume, countries, segment match rates, attributable sales and revenue
remain unknown. This is a commercial proposal, not an executed agreement.

## Concrete offer: AEKR contextual integration introduction

One named tools/integration supplier sponsors a clearly disclosed contextual
card on an agreed public AEKR category for 30 days. Its content is chosen from
the current page/category. Report only authorized valid impressions, clicks and
requested introductions, with their definitions and dates. A visitor may ask
that specific named supplier to contact them; show the recipient and exact
fields before submission. Proposed first-version fields are contact email,
chosen product/category and the recorded request permission. Do not send
private app content or share that lead with any other partner.

The first delivery can be demonstrated end-to-end with a synthetic supplier and
contact. Real activation remains dependent on supplier identity, notice,
agreement, allowed countries, delivery endpoint, suppression procedure and
price. A fixed sponsorship fee and optional accepted-lead tariff are separate
contract fields and currently **unknown**, not zero-price agreements. Lead
acceptance, invoice and collection are separate backend facts.

For owner review, resolve together: supplier identity/contact, category and
placement, 30-day dates, exact lead fields/recipient, eligibility and duplicate
criteria, rejection deadline, fixed fee/accepted-lead tariff, tax and invoicing
terms, payment deadline, retention and deletion acknowledgements. This packet
does not authorize contacting the supplier.

## Stack and economics

Reuse the existing Worker, D1, owner identity and central Analytics. A new CDP,
CMP service or distribution platform adds operational cost without a demonstrated
current need. Add a network-approved CMP only if a selected network requires it.
No service price, free entitlement or future invoice ceiling is assumed.

Use these distinct quantities: valid monetized impressions / 1,000 × net RPM
with the same denominator; accepted affiliate conversions × net commission;
valid authorized accepted leads × agreed tariff; segment billable use × tariff
or agreed fixed license. If starting from eligible impressions, include the
monetization rate. Cash margin is collected income minus acquisition, platform,
infrastructure and operation costs. At present all commercial outcome and cost
inputs are unknown; no forecast or user valuation is produced.
